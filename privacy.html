Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

TruthLens is a food label scanner. This page explains what data we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google. Sign-in is handled by Supabase.
  • Usage count: we store how many scans you have used and your plan (free or paid).
  • Label photos: a photo you scan is sent to an AI service (currently Google Gemini) only to read the nutrition values. Our server does not save the photo.

How we use it

To sign you in, count your free scans, read labels and show your safe amount. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (login and database)
  • Render (website hosting)
  • Google (sign-in and Gemini label reading)

Cookies and browser storage

Your browser keeps a sign-in session so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

To see or delete your data, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will delete your account and scan count on request.

Children

TruthLens is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
