Limits and sources

The numbers TruthLens uses, where they come from, and what they mean.

Fat, saturated fat and sugar levels come from an FSSAI note on high fat, sugar and salt foods. The sodium low level comes from a secondary source. The high cut-offs for sodium and trans fat are TruthLens choices, because FSSAI has not finalised its warning thresholds.

FSSAI label claims (per 100 g)

  • Low sugar: 5 g or less. Sugar free: 0.5 g or less.
  • Low fat: 3 g or less. Fat free: 0.5 g or less.
  • Trans fat free: under 0.2 g.

Things to know

  • FSSAI told the Supreme Court in August 2026 that it proposes red warning labels for high sugar, saturated fat and salt. Final rules are not published yet.
  • Labels show total sugars, but limits apply to added sugars. Use the added sugar line when it is printed.
  • Labels allow about 20% tolerance, so scanned values are approximate.

Sources: WHO sugars, saturated and trans fat, total fat and sodium guidelines; ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024; FSSAI Advertising and Claims regulations. General nutrition guidance, not medical advice.
