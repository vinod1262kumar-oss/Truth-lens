Questions

What does TruthLens do?

TruthLens reads the nutrition table on a packaged food and tells you how many grams are safe to eat today, based on WHO daily limits for sugar, saturated fat, trans fat, sodium and total fat.

How is the safe amount calculated?

For each nutrient, safe grams = daily limit divided by (nutrient per 100 g divided by 100). The smallest result is the limiting nutrient.

Which daily limits are used?

For a 2,000 kcal adult: sugar 25 g, saturated fat 22 g, trans fat 2.2 g, total fat 67 g and sodium 2,000 mg per day, based on WHO guidance. See all limits and sources.

See an example

A snack has 30 g sugar, 8 g saturated fat and 400 mg sodium per 100 g. Sugar allows 25 / 0.30 = about 83 g, saturated fat about 275 g and sodium 500 g. The smallest is 83 g, so sugar is the limiting nutrient and you should stop at about 80 g.
