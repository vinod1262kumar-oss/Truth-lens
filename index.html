How it works

1

Scan or type

Take a photo of the label, or type the values from the pack.

2

We compare

Each nutrient is checked against the daily limit and FSSAI style low and high levels.

3

You get a clear answer

A safe amount in grams, servings, and which nutrient runs out first.

Example

A snack has 30 g sugar, 8 g saturated fat and 400 mg sodium per 100 g. Sugar allows 25 / 0.30 = about 83 g, saturated fat about 275 g, sodium 500 g. The smallest is 83 g, so sugar is the limiting nutrient and you should stop at about 80 g, with no other sugary food that day.

Limits we use

  • Sugar: 25 g a day (WHO: under 5% of energy for extra benefit).
  • Saturated fat: 22 g a day (WHO: under 10% of energy).
  • Sodium: under 2,000 mg a day, about 5 g salt (WHO).
